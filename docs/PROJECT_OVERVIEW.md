# Project Overview - Scam Tactic Detector

## 1. Executive Summary
**Scam Tactic Detector** is an explainable natural language processing (NLP) application engineered to analyze investment dialogues and identify predatory psychological manipulation tactics. While traditional fraud detection systems focus on technical artifacts (e.g. IP addresses, domain age, malware signatures), scammers primarily deceive victims using cognitive and psychological exploits. 

The Scam Tactic Detector bridges this critical gap by providing real-time linguistic and cognitive telemetry to evaluate messages, extract evidence, compute heuristic risk scores, and deliver actionable defensive advisories.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Global financial fraud losses exceed billions annually, driven largely by retail investment scams, impersonation schemes, and high-yield investment programs (HYIPs). Fraudsters systematically exploit cognitive biases:
- **Urgency & Scarcity:** Forcing rapid capital commitment before due diligence can occur.
- **Authority Bias:** Falsely invoking regulatory or institutional prestige to establish unquestioned trust.
- **Greed & Asymmetry:** Offering guaranteed, risk-free profits that defy economic reality.
- **Herd Behavior / Social Proof:** Fabricating community consensus to overcome natural skepticism.
- **Sunk Cost Fallacy (Escalation):** Demanding advance withdrawal fees and escalation payments after initial deposits.

Retail investors lack real-time defensive tools to audit incoming communications before making irreversible financial transactions.

---

## 3. Target Audience
1. **Retail Investors & Crypto Traders:** Vulnerable individuals targeted across Telegram, WhatsApp, Discord, and social media.
2. **Financial Literacy Educators & Compliance Teams:** Organizations training consumers to recognize manipulation tactics.
3. **Customer Support & Fraud Desks:** Financial institutions evaluating suspicious customer withdrawal justifications or caller transcripts.
4. **Law Enforcement & Cyber Cells:** Investigators triaging fraud complaints and cataloging threat actor typologies.

---

## 4. Key Capabilities & Innovations
- **Multi-Tactic Detection:** Evaluates five distinct psychological manipulation dimensions simultaneously.
- **Evidence Extraction:** Pinpoints exact suspicious phrases and maps character offsets for in-line visualization.
- **Explainable AI (XAI):** Provides transparent, rule-based reasoning rather than black-box score predictions.
- **Context & Negation Awareness:** Suppresses false alarms on legitimate investment disclaimers, educational articles, and prudent financial planning.
- **Synergistic Risk Modeling:** Weighs toxic tactic combinations (e.g., Urgency + Guaranteed Returns + Authority Impersonation) non-linearly to reflect real-world coercion.
